# Signal Lab v1.1

The pink and blue Portal Pal characters and Aanya poses were visually matched against the user's `Visuals.zip` character sheets. The individual transparent crops in `assets/crew/` are from the Studio Bridge brand exports included in `first-90-days-exhibit3-v1.1.zip`. The original sheets are unchanged.

`index.html` contains the supplied exhibit and its editable formatted application code. `experience.css` contains the layout and motion refinements; `experience.js` contains locally synthesized audio, with a persistent sound toggle. No audio files or external audio requests are used. The character scenes are illustrative situations, not participant quotations.
